# Carter Pitch

Voice-controlled race starring Carter. Run up an angled track over your own camera feed and use your voice to change Carter's shape and get past 20 obstacles to the finish line:

- **Deep voice**: short and fat. Duck limbo, croc pits
- **High voice**: tall and slim. Cow squeeze, "you must be this tall"

Clean runs build speed. Hit something and Carter bonks backwards, loses most of his speed, and has to try that obstacle again. Fastest finish time is saved.

**Play it:** https://immortalbobik.github.io/carter-pitch/

Asks for camera + microphone on start. No mic? Hold ↓ / ↑ or tap the bottom / top half of the screen.

Single `index.html`, no build step.
