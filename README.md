# Carter Filters

TikTok-style camera filter games starring Carter. Open the page, pick a filter, play.

**Play it:** https://immortalbobik.github.io/carter-pitch/

## Carter Dodge
Three lanes over your camera. Tilt your head left, keep it straight or tilt right to be in the left, middle or right lane. Dodge falling stone Carter boulders, grab coins (combos multiply), and catch a star for 3 seconds of invincibility that smashes boulders. 60 seconds, 3 hearts, faster as it goes. No camera? Hold the left/right side of the screen or use the arrow keys.

## Carter Maze
Face tracking as a joystick: turn or nod your head and Carter rolls that way through a glowing maze drawn over your camera. Grab every key to unlock the door. Ghosts patrol short corridors and all fade out together every few seconds: touch a solid one and you're sent back to your last key (+3 s). 5 random mazes, each bigger than the last, fastest total time wins. No camera? Drag anywhere like a joystick or use the arrow keys.

## Carter Says
Simon Says with your face, using face tracking. Carter calls out a face (open wide, big smile, eyebrows up, wink, kissy face, close your eyes, tilt left/right) and you have to pull it before the timer runs out. If he doesn't say "Carter says" first, keep a straight face. It learns your neutral face at the start, so a resting smile doesn't count. 20 rounds, 3 hearts, faster every round, optional read-out-loud voice. No camera? Tap the matching face button.

## Carter Chomp
Face tracking (MediaPipe Face Landmarker, runs on your phone). Chef Carter wanders along the top and lobs food at you in arcs. Move your head to get under it and open your real mouth to eat it. Golden Carter heads are +50, combos multiply your points x2 / x3. Chili peppers, bombs and stinky socks cost a heart if they land in an open mouth, so shut it. 45 seconds, 3 hearts. No camera? Drag to move a cartoon mouth, hold to open it.

## Carter Pour
Cups roll past on a conveyor belt under a Carter soda bottle. Hold to pour, let go to stop, top up as often as you like, but only while the cup is underneath: pour onto the belt and you lose points. The bottle glugs and pours harder the longer you hold, drink still in the air keeps landing, and every cup is a different shape (cone, flask, hourglass, vase...) so the level rises at different speeds. Later cups are frosted so you can barely see inside. 10 cups, up to 100 points each, overflow = 0.

## Carter Stack
Carter's face is cut into 15 horizontal slices. Each one slides back and forth over the stack. Tap to drop it, and whatever hangs over the edge gets chopped off, so the next slice is thinner. Land it dead on for a PERFECT (3 in a row grows the slice back a bit). Miss completely and Carter falls. Stack all 15 to rebuild his whole face.

## Balloon Pop
20 Carter balloons stacked into a tower, each one smaller and thinner than the last. A needle swings back and forth through 180°. Tap to fire: it flies dead straight and pops every balloon it crosses, so the trajectory is everything. Straight up takes the whole tower in one shot, a few degrees off pops only the bottom few. What's left drops down for the next needle. 8 needles to clear all 20. Best = fewest needles.

## Voice Race
Run up an angled track over your camera feed. A deep voice makes Carter short and fat (duck limbo, croc pits), a high voice makes him tall and slim (cow squeeze, "you must be this tall"). Clean runs build speed. A bonk knocks you back and slows you down. 20 obstacles to the finish line.

All filters ask for the camera (in HD, up to 1080p) (Voice Race also asks for the mic). Nothing is recorded or uploaded. Plain HTML, no build step.
